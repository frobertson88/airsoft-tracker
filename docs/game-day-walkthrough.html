    WHAT TO LOOK FOR

    Clear at a glance.
    Detail on demand.

    At each step, can you tell what is happening, what you can do and what happens next?

    Player

    Can I join easily? Am I in play? Who helps me? Did my action succeed?

    Host

    What blocks the start? Who is waiting? What will this change do to players?

    When things go wrong

    Is the status honest? Is there a clear way forward without repeating setup?

    Review panels and scenarios are proposed presentation choices. Pending-action conflict rules, field editing after start and real device behaviour remain implementation work.
